The workshop

Video game design

Paint the level, change the rules and press Play. When you like it, export it: you take away a real game in a single file that works in any browser, on phones too.

What you can make here

  • A platform game
  • A maze seen from above
  • A race against the clock
  • Your game in an HTML file you can share

How it works

  1. Choose a tool below (Ground, Platform, Coin, Hazard, Goal, Enemy, Player) and paint by tapping or dragging on the level.
  2. Press Play. You play with the arrow keys and the space bar (or the on-screen buttons). Press Stop the game to go back to editing.
  3. Under Properties you change the kind of game, the goal, speed, jump, gravity, lives and time.
  4. The Structure list counts what is in the level and warns you if something is missing, such as the player or the goal.
  5. Export the playable game (HTML) or an image of the level from File.

This studio is an interactive tool and needs JavaScript. Below you will find the full explanation of how it works and what you learn.

What each tile does

Ground: solid on every side. Platform: you can pass through it from below and stand on it from above. Ice: slippery. Hazard: costs a life. Coin: you collect it. Goal: ends the level. Spring: jumps very high. Enemy: walks back and forth; if you jump on it, it disappears. Key and door: the door opens once you have the key.

Designing a good level

Show each idea first somewhere safe, then ask for it again with more difficulty. Let players see the goal or a coin before a blind jump. Play your level many times: if it frustrates you, it will frustrate others.

A game that can be played in many ways

The exported game works with a keyboard or with on-screen buttons on touch screens, announces coins, lives and the ending to screen readers, pauses with P or Esc and has no flashing.

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the workshop does not store it in the browser.

To keep it, open File and press “Save project”: a file downloads to your device. To carry on another day, open File, press “Open…” and choose that file. Exports (images, sound, 3D models, games) are also files that you download yourself.

How it is made

Libraries are served from irisgreen.eu, with no outside services, and they only load in the studio that uses them. Licences: PixiJS (MIT) in the editor; the exported game uses only Iris Green’s own engine, with no libraries · full list of licences.
