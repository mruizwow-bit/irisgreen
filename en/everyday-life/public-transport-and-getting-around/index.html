Public transport and getting around

Going outDRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; the final check is still pending.

Moving around the city combines timetables, orientation, noise, crowds, platform changes, tickets and quick decisions. The barrier may be in one part of the journey, not in “using transport” in general.

Before leaving

Prepare the route with a clear starting point and end point, save a screenshot of the journey, identify an alternative if the service is cancelled and carry enough battery. For someone who needs it, a visual sequence of five or six steps may be more useful than a long explanation.

What can be requested

Accessible information, help locating a stop or platform, time to understand an instruction and reasonable access measures where there is a disability. Ley 15/2022 expressly includes transport among the areas protected against discrimination.

If travelling alone is not yet safe

Practise one section at a time: first accompanied, then with agreed follow-up and finally independently if the person wants to and can. Autonomy does not mean withdrawing support too soon.

Where it is written

  • Ley 15/2022
  • Ley General de discapacidad
