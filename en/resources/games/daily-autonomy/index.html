Daily living · real practice

Everyday tasks, step by step

Here you can practise tasks that may take a lot of effort. There is no score, time limit or single correct way to learn.

Choose one task. You can look at the steps, try one, stop, and come back another day. If a sequence does not work for you, change it. Asking for help is also part of doing a task.

Shoelaces · coordination and sequence

Tying shoelaces

This activity practises one common way to make a bow. It is not the only valid method. You can practise with the shoe off your foot if that is easier.

    Adult life · home

    Using a washing machine

    Use these steps as a flexible checklist. Machines, clothes and laundry products can have different instructions.

    1. Check pockets and clothing labels when needed.
    2. Put the clothes in without overloading the machine.
    3. Add the product specified for that machine, following its label.
    4. Choose a programme that suits the clothes.
    5. Start the machine.
    6. When it finishes, remove the clothes and dry them as appropriate.

    Safety: do not mix cleaning products. If you do not know a machine or product, follow its label or ask for help before using it.
