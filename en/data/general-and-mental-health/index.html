What it measures

The categories of ‘excellent’ general health and mental health come from the survey instrument. The comparison shows a difference between groups, but it is not equivalent to counting medical or psychiatric diagnoses.

How to cite it

‘Excellent health’ and ‘excellent mental health’ are categories from the survey instrument. They are not equivalent to the absence or presence of a clinical diagnosis.

What to avoid

These are survey results and the assessment of ‘excellent’ depends on the instrument used. They are not equivalent to disease diagnoses.
