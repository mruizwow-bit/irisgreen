“I feel I have to hide parts of myself to fit in”

Daily life

This page describes an everyday situation, not a diagnosis. The same situation can have several explanations, and a professional assessment is what distinguishes between them.

In brief

This is not about performing in general but hiding specific things: an interest you could talk about for hours, a movement that helps you regulate, or the need to leave earlier. People often hide these things because showing them went badly somewhere before.

It does not automatically mean

  • Hiding interests, movements or needs may be a self-protective strategy, but it is not automatically autistic masking or a diagnostic sign.

What to look at

  • What exactly is being hidden and since when.

What may help now

  • Choose one thing and one safer setting, and try there first if you want to.

What to avoid

  • Revealing everything at once in the setting where it previously went badly.

When to seek professional help

It may be useful to discuss this with a trusted person or professional if hiding is affecting mental health, identity, work, education or relationships. The safety of the setting is part of the decision.

Warning signs

If there is violence, coercion, an immediate threat or a risk of self-harm, prioritise safety and seek urgent help. In Spain, the 024 helpline can be used for suicide risk and 112 for a life-threatening emergency.
