Preparing a conversation about name, pronouns, privacy and boundaries

You can decide what you want to ask for, who you want to tell and how much you want to explain. Being neurodivergent does not invalidate an identity or orientation, and asking to be treated respectfully does not require you to tell your whole story.

This tool is for preparing a conversation or message. It does not assume that you want to disclose your identity or that you have to.

1 · Decide what you need now

2 · Choose how to say it

It can be in person, by message, by email or in a note. Written communication can give you more time to organise what you want to say.

One format is not more “brave” than another.

3 · Short phrases you can adapt

“Please use the name ___ for me.”
“You can tell ___; please do not tell other people.”
“I do not want to explain more about this now.”
“If you make a mistake, correct it and move on.”
“I need this in writing.”

4 · Afterwards

It may help to keep the message you sent, write down what was agreed, or have a trusted person present. You can also change what you want to share later.
