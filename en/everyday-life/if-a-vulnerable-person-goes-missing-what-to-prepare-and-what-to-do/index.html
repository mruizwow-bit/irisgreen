If a vulnerable person goes missing: what to prepare and what to do

RightsDRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; the final check is still pending.

In a concerning disappearance, there is no need to wait 24 hours. The response must be adapted to the age, health, communication, autonomy and circumstances of the missing person.

Beforehand: a prepared sheet

Recent photo, physical description, important medication, communication method, usual places, contacts, vehicle registration or transport if relevant, and any information that could guide the search. Update it when the photo or information changes.

At the time

Call 112 if there is immediate risk and report the disappearance without delay to the National Police or Guardia Civil. Bring the photo and available information. Do not delay the report because of the idea that 24 hours must pass.

Technology

AlertCops allows chat with the Police/Guardia Civil, an SOS button and location sharing with contacts; some “Guardian” functions are activated for specific services or circumstances. It does not replace reporting the disappearance and should not be presented as a permanent location system for another person without their knowledge.

The II Strategic Plan on Missing Persons is in force from 2026 to 2029, with 5.1 million in funding, five lines of action, thirteen objectives and 102 measures.
