Australian Bureau of Statistics · Survey of Disability, Ageing and Carers 2022:

The figures

290,900 people
1.1% of the Australian population

In 2018 there were 205,200 people, 0.8%.

ABS records a 41.8% increase in the estimated number between the two surveys.

By age

What it measures

ABS estimates the autistic population through the Survey of Disability, Ageing and Carers 2022. The breakdowns by sex and age are part of the same survey; comparison with 2018 shows change between rounds, not its cause.

How to cite it

The figure comes from the SDAC 2022 survey. The increase compared with 2018 should not automatically be interpreted as an increase in incidence.

What to avoid

The increase between surveys may reflect changes in identification, diagnosis, population composition and reporting. It should not automatically be interpreted as a biological change in incidence.
