Accessible reading

Open Reading in the header to adjust the presentation. You can combine the options and return to the original view. This page explains what the available controls do and what limitations remain.

What each button does

A− and A+ · Text size

They change the size of the content between 100%, 115%, 130% and 150%. The percentage shows the selected value and the button is disabled when it reaches its limit. The header and floating panels are not enlarged by this setting. You can also use your browser zoom.

Wider letter spacing

Applies a combination of spacing between letters, words and lines. To adjust them separately, open “Typography, spacing and width”, which also lets you choose paragraph spacing. Using this combined button replaces custom spacing settings; it does not change the selected typeface or width.

Bigger buttons

Increases the text controls that are prepared for this setting. It does not enlarge areas overlaid on illustrations, because they could cover each other. In Vera and the home map, you can open “Use buttons instead of touching the image”.

More contrast

Strengthens text and reading backgrounds without applying a colour filter to photographs or illustrations. It is not a full theme selector and does not guarantee that every graphic element is adapted.

Reading guide

Shows a band that follows the pointer. If keyboard focus coincides with the band, it moves away from the control. The guide still does not have its own controls for positioning it with the keyboard or through touch buttons.

Read aloud

Activates the voice function available on the page. On text pages, it starts when you press a fragment; some interactive pages use a prepared reading text. Turn the button off to stop it. This function does not replace a screen reader and still does not offer pause, resume or speed controls.

It uses the voice service provided by the browser. Some voices may use remote services: there is no guarantee that all text processing is local. Reloading or changing page does not start a voice because other preferences have been saved.

Reduce motion

Disables the page’s animations and transitions controlled by its styles. If your device requests reduced motion, the website respects that even when this own option is switched off. The panel indicates when that device preference is active. This setting does not control playback inside external video services.

Reset

Resets only Iris Green’s reading preferences. It does not delete favourites or the chosen language. It continues to respect reduced motion and forced colours on your device.

Typography, spacing and width

Open Reading and expand “Typography, spacing and width”. You can choose the original typeface or Arial-, Verdana- or Georgia-style alternatives, depending on the fonts available on your device. No new typeface is downloaded and no promise is made that one font is better for everyone.

Spacing between letters, words, lines and paragraphs has four independent controls. Values marked with × indicate a proportion of the font size. “Original” restores the usual presentation for that setting; it does not modify the others. The quick spacing button still offers a combination and replaces the four custom spacing settings when you use it.

Medium or Narrow width limits the length of text lines without reducing the chosen size. “Reset these settings” restores the original typeface, spacing and width; it keeps size, contrast and the other preferences. “Reset”, outside that block, still resets all the website’s own preferences.

Your choices are kept in the same browser when it allows them to be saved. The sample in the panel lets you compare the typeface and spacing. Header, panels and illustrations keep their presentation; pages and boards still need their own usability checks. These settings are not an accessibility certification or a replacement for assistive technologies.

Saving the settings

Presentation preferences are saved in this browser and restored when you move between pages connected to the reading system. No account is needed. If the browser does not allow them to be saved, they work on the current page and the panel warns you about that limitation. Deleting site data or using another browser may mean you need to choose them again.

Colours and selected controls

The selected shared filters have underlined text, as well as the colour change. When the browser activates a forced-colours palette, its colours are used for controls, fields and panels. There is no need to activate a “colour-blind mode”. The review of information conveyed by colour in all graphics and games is not finished.

Music and keyboard

Music opens a small player at the bottom right. It does not start playing when you open it: playback starts when you press Listen, choose a track or use Previous or Next. You can close it with Escape or its close button. If the player or the Reading panel covers a control that receives keyboard focus outside the panel, it collapses to leave it visible. Collapsing the player does not stop a track you have already started; open Music again to pause it.

Scope of the checks

Technical tests of controls and navigation have been carried out in a test browser, at desktop and mobile sizes, and with emulated device preferences. They are not the same as testing every device, every voice or every combination of settings.

Testing with people who use screen readers, braille displays and voice control is still pending. A guide usable without a mouse, full themes, advanced read-aloud controls and text alternatives for some materials are still pending. The actual availability of alternative fonts depends on the device. No full WCAG certification is declared.

Report a difficulty

Write to informacion@irisgreen.eu indicating the page, what you were trying to do, the browser or device and the settings used. You do not need to provide a diagnosis.
