Privacy

This page explains what information may be stored when you use Iris Green and how you can delete it.

Iris Green does not use user accounts, advertising or analytics tools to track what you do on the website.

Information stored in your browser

The reading preferences you choose may be stored in your browser so that you do not have to set them again on every page.

Other website preferences, such as your selected language or favourites, may also be stored in your browser when you use those features.

You do not need to create an account.

Contact by email

The website does not use a contact form.

If you email Iris Green, we receive the email address you use and any information you include in your message so that we can reply.

YouTube videos

Videos are not loaded until you choose to play them.

When you load a video, your browser connects to YouTube. Iris Green uses YouTube’s Privacy Enhanced Mode.

From that point, YouTube’s terms and privacy policy also apply.

Spotify music

Spotify is not loaded until you open the music feature.

When the player loads, your browser connects to Spotify and that service’s privacy terms apply.

Read aloud

The Read aloud feature uses the speech service available in your browser.

Depending on the browser, device and voice you choose, processing may take place on your device or through a remote service.

Typefaces

The website uses Newsreader and Atkinson Hyperlegible.

These typefaces are currently loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore connects to Google’s servers to request the font files.

How to delete information stored in your browser

To restore the original reading settings, select Reset in the Reading panel.

This button only resets your reading preferences.

To remove all data stored locally by irisgreen.eu in that browser, use your browser’s privacy settings to delete the site’s data.

Privacy contact

If you have a question about privacy or about information you have sent by email, contact informacion@irisgreen.eu.

If the website changes

If Iris Green introduces new features that collect or store information, this page will be updated to explain what information is used and why.
