The workshop · Studio 25

Ideas and inventions studio

From a loose idea to an invention you can build. Warm up with the six tables, transform objects with SCAMPER and design inventions with real limits on money, size and weight.

How it works

  1. Warm up at one of the six tables if you like. You can skip it.
  2. Pick a challenge: SCAMPER to transform an object, or a commission with limits.
  3. On the invention sheet, write the problem, who it is for and how it is used. Add the parts with their price and weight.
  4. The “How the sheet is going” list updates itself: it says what is met and what is missing.
  5. Save the project to a file or print the sheet.

The tool needs JavaScript. Here are all the challenges so you can do them on paper or with another tool.

Challenges

The challenges are grouped by level. You can start with any of them.

Level 1 · Looking differently

  1. A clothes peg. Take a clothes peg through the seven questions of the SCAMPER method. One idea for each letter.
  2. Three ideas per letter. Choose an object and write three ideas for each SCAMPER letter: twenty-one ideas. One per line.

Level 2 · Small commissions

  1. Phone stand. A stand that holds a phone upright so you can follow a recipe while cooking.
  2. Never forget the keys. Something by the door that makes it hard to leave home without your keys.

Level 3 · Real commissions

  1. Reading in bed. Read lying down for a long time without tiring your arms or neck.
  2. A plant without you for a week. Keep a potted plant watered for seven days without anyone watering it.
  3. Tidy cables. Stop the cables on a desk from tangling or falling on the floor, so you can unplug one without moving the rest.

Level 4 · No ceiling

  1. Random commission. A random problem, person, place and set of limits. Ask for another commission whenever you like: they never run out.

Why limits

Well-chosen limits help you invent: they push you to look for routes you would never try with endless money and space. That is why each commission has a maximum budget, size and weight. Limits never come with a score or a clock.

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the website does not keep it.

To carry on another day, press “Save file”: a file with your project downloads to your device. To pick it up again, press “Open file” and choose it. If you close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser warns you.
