Neurodiversity and disability in figures

There is no single official figure for ‘neurodivergent people worldwide’. Major international organisations do not use neurodivergence as a homogeneous statistical category: they measure specific conditions, disability, functioning, support needs, diagnosis, identification or social participation.

This section brings together figures that can be cited with precision. Each page states what was measured, in whom, where, when and using which source. When two figures seem contradictory, the first step is to check whether they are really measuring the same thing.

The 49 pages in this section are mounted. All are in draft: the source is named and linked, and final verification is still pending.
