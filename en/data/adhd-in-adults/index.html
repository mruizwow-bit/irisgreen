ADHD in adults

WorldData from estructura demográfica mundial de 2020DRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; final verification is still pending.

An international meta-analysis estimated a 2020 prevalence of 2.58% for adult ADHD persisting from childhood, equivalent to about 139.84 million adults. When a definition of symptomatic adult ADHD was used without requiring proof of childhood onset, the estimate was 6.76%, about 366.33 million. These are different definitions and should not be presented as a single figure.

The figures

There are two different figures and they should not be mixed.

2,58 %adult ADHD persisting from childhood

Estimate adjusted to the 2020 global demographic structure:

139.84 million adults
6,76 %symptomatic adult ADHD, regardless of proof of childhood onset

Equivalent to:

366.33 million adults

What it measures

The meta-analysis builds two estimates because it uses two definitions of adult ADHD. One requires persistence from childhood and the other is based on adult symptoms without requiring proof of that onset; this is why the percentages and resulting millions are different.

How to cite it

Keep the two definitions separate: ADHD persisting from childhood (2.58%) and symptomatic adult ADHD (6.76%). Do not add or average the two figures.

What to avoid

The figure must remain linked to its population, territory, year and method. Separating it from that context can change its meaning.
