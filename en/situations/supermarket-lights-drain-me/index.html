“I leave the supermarket exhausted and I don't know why”

Sensory experiences

This page describes an everyday situation, not a diagnosis. The same situation can have several explanations, and a professional assessment is what distinguishes between them.

In brief

Bright light, flicker, music and voices add up. The body gets through the shopping trip and the tiredness can hit afterwards, all at once.

It does not automatically mean

  • Leaving a supermarket exhausted is not enough on its own to conclude that sensory overload or autism is involved.

What to look at

  • How long it takes before the discomfort starts.

What may help now

  • Go at quieter times and use a finished shopping list.

What to avoid

  • Pushing through to the end ‘since we're already here’.

When to seek professional help

Seek an assessment if light triggers repeated headaches, nausea, aura, visual changes or substantial avoidance of activities you need to do. You do not need to reach a specific number of episodes before asking for help if it is limiting your life.

Warning signs

A sudden headache that reaches maximum intensity within minutes, new loss of vision, weakness, confusion or a new speech problem needs urgent medical attention.
