Want to make your own? Visual routines

You need JavaScript to create the sheets. These are the routines:

  • The morning before going out: Wake up, Get up, Go to the toilet, Brush your teeth, Get dressed, Have breakfast, Take your backpack, Leave home.
  • The evening before bed: Have dinner, Brush your teeth, Put on pyjamas, Turn off the screen, Leave just a low light, Turn off the light and sleep.
  • Brushing your teeth: Pick up the toothbrush, Put on toothpaste, Brush your teeth, Rinse with water, Dry your mouth.
  • Washing your hands: Turn on the water, Soap, Rub your hands, Rinse with water, Turn off the tap, Dry your hands.
  • Having a shower: Get undressed, Turn on the water, Get wet in the shower, Use shower gel, Wash your hair, Rinse off, Dry off with the towel, Get dressed.
  • Going to the toilet: Go to the toilet, Toilet paper, Flush the toilet, Wash your hands, Dry your hands.
  • Shaving: Apply shaving foam, Shave downwards, Rinse your face, Dry your face.
  • Cutting your nails: Position the nail clippers, Clip, File.
  • Dressing for rain: It is raining today, Raincoat, Boots, Take the umbrella, Leave home.
  • Tying your laces: Cross the two laces, Pass one underneath and pull, Make a loop with one lace, Wrap the other lace around the loop, Push the tip through the gap and pull.
  • Doing up buttons: Look at the button and the buttonhole, Push the button through the hole, Repeat with the next one.
  • Packing your school bag: Look at the timetable, Folder, Pencil case, Packed lunch, Water bottle, Take your backpack.
  • Ready the night before: Lay out your clothes, Pack your bag, Charge your phone, Keys in their place, Lunch ready, Set the alarm.
  • A simple breakfast: Get out the bowl, Pour the cereal, Pour the milk, Take a spoon, Have breakfast.
  • Making a sandwich: Get out the bread, Carefully open the bread, Add the filling, Close the sandwich, Put it in the lunch box.
  • Cooking a recipe: Read the recipe, Get the ingredients out, Cook, Serve it on the plate, Tidy the kitchen.
  • Setting and clearing the table: Set the table, Eat, Clear and wipe the table.
  • Doing the washing: Separate clean and dirty clothes, Put the clothes in the machine, Add the detergent, Switch it on, Hang the washing, Add the pegs.
  • Folding clothes: Lay the garment flat, Fold the sleeves in, Fold it in half, Make a pile, Put it in the wardrobe.
  • Cleaning the house in parts: Wash the dishes, Dust, Vacuum, Clean the bathroom, Take out the rubbish.
  • Doing the shopping: Write the list, Local shop, Pick up what is on the list, I join the queue, Pay, I put the shopping in the bag, Put the shopping away.
  • Crossing the road: Reach the zebra crossing, Stop at the kerb, Look at the lights, Look both ways, Walk across.
  • Taking the bus: Home, Go out of the door, Bus stop, Bus, Arrive.
  • Taking the train: Validate your ticket, Wait on the platform, Get on the train, Check the stops, Get off at your stop.
  • Going to a medical appointment: Health card, Home, Waiting room, Consulting room, Something calm.
  • Making a phone call: Note down what you will say, Find the number, Breathe before calling, Call and say it, Write down the answer.
  • Answering an email: Read the email, Find what they are asking, Write a short reply, Check it once, Send it.
  • Studying in parts: Check what the test covers, Cut the task into pieces, Study one piece, Take a break, Go over what you studied.
  • Starting a task: Look at the timetable, Tidy the desk, Cut the task into pieces, Do one small piece, Take a break.
  • Getting home after an exhausting day: Get home, Take your shoes off, Some quiet time, Eat something simple, Go to bed early.
  • Put on and take off shoes: Sit down, Take your shoes, Put on one shoe, Put on the other shoe, Stand up.
  • Do up and undo a zip: Join the two parts at the bottom, Hold the bottom, Pull the zip up, Pull the zip down.
  • Choose your clothes: Check the weather, Check what you are doing today, Choose the top, Choose the bottom, Choose the shoes, Put it all together.
  • Get dressed in order: Underwear, Socks, Bottoms, Top, Shoes, Dressed.
  • Lay out tomorrow's clothes: Check tomorrow, Take out the clothes, Put it on a chair, Put the shoes underneath, Finished.
  • Undress and put clothes away: Take off your clothes, Sort clean from dirty, Dirty clothes in the basket, Clean clothes in the wardrobe.
  • Wash your hair: Wet your hair, Put on shampoo, Rub, Rinse, Dry your hair.
  • Dry yourself: Take the towel, Dry your face, Dry your body, Dry your hair, Hang the towel.
  • Brush your hair: Take the hairbrush, Brush your hair, Look in the mirror, Finished.
  • Period care: Check the calendar, Carry a pad or tampon, Change in the toilet, Put it in the bin, Wash your hands.
  • Shave or personal grooming: Get what you need, Wet your skin, Shave, Rinse, Dry.
  • Use deodorant: Take the deodorant, Put it on, Put it away.
  • Get up: The alarm goes off, Wake up, Sit up in bed, Get up, Open the curtains.
  • Have breakfast: Go to the kitchen, Make breakfast, Eat, Drink, Clear up.
  • Check keys, phone and documents: Keys, Phone, Wallet, Travel pass, Finished.
  • Put on your coat and leave: Check the weather, Put on your coat, Take your bag, Open the door, Go out.
  • Leave on time: See the leaving time, Set a timer, First: the essentials, Then: the optional, Leave.
  • Transition home → school or work: Leave home, Walk or take transport, Arrive, Put your things away, Start.
  • Make a simple breakfast: Get what you need, Put cereal in the bowl, Add the milk, Take a spoon, Eat, Clear up.
  • Set the table: Plate, Fork, Knife, Glass, Napkin.
  • Clear the table: Take the plates, Scrape the leftovers, Wipe the table, Finished.
  • Wash up: Fill the sink, Add washing-up liquid, Wash up, Rinse, Dry and put away.
  • Put food away: Cover the food, Open the fridge, Put it away, Close the fridge.
  • Use the microwave safely: Put the food on a plate, Put it in the microwave, Set the time, Wait, Take it out carefully: it is hot, Eat.
  • Follow a simple visual recipe: Look at all the steps, Get the ingredients, Do step one, Do the next step, Finish.
  • Pack a packed lunch: Take the lunch box, Put in the food, Put in the fruit, Put in the drink, Close it.
  • Drink water through the day: Fill your bottle, Drink in the morning, Drink at midday, Drink in the afternoon.
  • Make the bed: Straighten the sheet, Pull up the duvet, Put the pillow in place, Finished.
  • Tidy a room: Choose one area, Clothes in the basket, Rubbish in the bin, Everything in its place, Look at the result.
  • Sort the laundry: Whites, Colours, Delicates, Each pile in its place.
  • Hang out the washing: Take out the washing, Shake it out, Hang it up, Use the pegs.
  • Put things back in their place: Pick up what is out of place, Look where it goes, Put it away.
  • Take out the rubbish: Tie the bag, Take it out of the bin, Take it outside, Put in a new bag.
  • Sort the recycling: Paper, Plastic, Glass, Food waste, Take it to the bins.
  • Clean a surface: Take things off, Take the cloth, Wipe it, Put things back.
  • Vacuum: Use ear protection if you need it, Pick things up off the floor, Vacuum, Put the vacuum away.
  • Water the plants: Fill the watering can, Water the plants, Put it away.
  • Look after a pet: Feed the animal, Give water, Walk or clean, Finished.
  • Set up your desk: Clear what you do not need, Put out your materials, Put water there, Silence notifications.
  • Break a big task down: Show the whole task, Cut it into pieces, Put the pieces in order, Do one, Tick it.
  • Put your materials away: Close the books, Put away the pencil case, Leave the desk clear.
  • Hand in a task: Check it is complete, Put your name on it, Take it or send it, Check it arrived.
  • Transition break → work: Warning that the break is ending, Put away the break things, Drink water, Sit down, One small first step.
  • Ask for an adjustment or help: Choose who to ask, Choose the moment, Say what you need, Write down what you agreed.
  • Follow a day timetable: Look at the timetable, First, Next, Break, Finished.
  • Cross at the crossing: Go to the crossing, Stop at the kerb, Look at the lights, Look both ways, Walk across.
  • Wait your turn: Join the queue, Wait, Move when the queue moves, Your turn.
  • Use public transport: Check the line and the stop, Go to the stop, Wait, Use your ticket, Sit or hold on, Get off at your stop.
  • Get on and off the bus or metro: Let people off first, Get on, Tap your card, Find a place, Get off.
  • Shop in a shop: Take your list, Take a basket, Find the items, Go to the till, Pay, Pack your shopping.
  • Pay: Take out your card or money, Look at the amount, Pay, Take the change, Put your wallet away.
  • Keep the receipt and change: Take the receipt, Put it in one fixed place, Put the change away.
  • Ask for help: Find someone who works there, Show your card or symbol, Say what you need, Say thank you.
  • Find your way in a familiar place: Find a landmark, Look at the map or arrows, Walk towards it, Check it is the right place.
  • Leave when you are overloaded: Notice the body signal, Tell someone or show your card, Go somewhere quiet, Put on your headphones, Wait until you feel better.
  • Get ready to go out: Warning: ten minutes left, Finish what you are doing, Take your things, Go out.
  • Finish an activity: Warning that it is ending, Put it away, Finished, What comes next.
  • Switch task: Stop, Put away the last task, Look at what comes next, Get out the new thing, Start.
  • Wait: See how long is left, Choose something to do while you wait, Wait, Now it is time.
  • Follow a visual countdown: See the whole time, Watch it go down, Final warning, Finished.
