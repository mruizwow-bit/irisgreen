My Iris Card

A card with three things about you, to show when saying it out loud is hard work: at an appointment, at a counter, at work, in class.

What you write stays in your browser while the tab is open. Close it and nothing is left. It is not sent anywhere and no account is needed.

Write your own

You do not have to fill it all in at once, or use all three parts. One sentence is already enough.

Right now there is an example written in, so you can see how it looks. You can write over it or clear it.

Shape of the card

All three say the same thing. Pick the one that is easiest for you to read and to show.

Which situation is hard for you. No need to explain why.

A symbol, if you want one

What makes it easier for you.

What helps me, in this order

Three things, in the order that works for you. You can leave one blank.

    What you need from the other person, said in the positive.

    A symbol, if you want one

    Your card

    It builds as you write. This is how it looks when you show it, and how it prints.

    The card as text

    This is what gets copied. Use it to paste into a message, an email or a note on your phone.
