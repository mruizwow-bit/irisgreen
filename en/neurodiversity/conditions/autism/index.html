Autism

development

Description

Autism is a neurodevelopmental condition. It can involve differences in communication and social interaction, repeated movements or activities, a need for predictability and particular sensory responses. It is present from early development, even when recognised in adulthood. Abilities and support needs differ between people and can change over time.

For children and young people, assessment brings together developmental history, observation of communication and behaviour, health, and experiences at home and school. It aims to understand strengths and needs as well as difficulties. It is not a matter of identifying one gesture or an isolated experience.

Frequency and uncertainty

The review by Zeidan and colleagues, published in 2022, brought together 71 studies, mainly of children: the median of their prevalence estimates was 1%. This is not a uniform figure for every country.

The GBD study estimated that 61.8 million people of all ages were autistic in 2021, approximately 1 in 127, with a 95% uncertainty interval of 52.1–72.7 million. This is a statistical estimate, not a direct count. It should not be compared with the previous figure as though both measured the same population in the same way.

What helps

Choose support with the person: understandable information, adjustments to light and noise, advance notice of changes and help with tasks where needed. In childhood, coordinate with family and school; in adulthood, also with work and everyday settings. Review the purpose and usefulness of each support individually.

When speech does not meet communication needs, augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) offers other ways to express oneself. It is selected around the person's abilities, preferences and settings of use.

What is not supported by evidence

Medication should not be presented as a way to remove autism. NICE advises against antipsychotics and antidepressants for its core features. This does not mean rejecting treatment for co-occurring conditions or other specific indications: pain, sleep, anxiety and other problems need their own assessment.
