The workshop · Studio 2

Graphic design studio

An artboard for real design work: shapes, texts in several typefaces, a colour palette and layers. The studio measures the contrast of each text against what is behind it and tells you whether it is easy to read.

  • Shapes, texts and layers
  • Live WCAG 2.2 contrast
  • Five formats
  • PNG and SVG
  • 10 challenges on 5 levels
  • Everything by keyboard

How it works

  1. Choose a format: poster, card, logo, sign or screen. Or choose a challenge.
  2. Add shapes and texts with the buttons above the artboard.
  3. Change size, colour, typeface and position in “Properties”, or drag on the artboard.
  4. Look at “Result” and the layers table: they show the contrast of each text.
  5. Save the project to a file, or download it as PNG or SVG.

The tool needs JavaScript. Here are all the challenges so you can do them on paper or with another tool.

Challenges

The challenges are grouped by level. You can start with any of them.

Level 1 · Shapes and letters

  1. A logo with one shape. Design a square logo with a single shape and a name. The name must be easy to read.
  2. Two-colour poster. A poster with only two colours (the background counts) and a big title.

Level 2 · Easy to read

  1. Fix the contrast. This poster is hard to read. Change colours until all three texts pass AA contrast.
  2. Important things first. Three texts with hierarchy: the biggest is at least twice the smallest, and none is below 14.

Level 3 · Layout

  1. All in line. Three or more texts aligned on the same edge (left, centre or right) and at least one shape.
  2. Business card. A card with a name, a job and a way to get in touch, with three colours at most.

Level 4 · Systems and signs

  1. Building sign. A sign that points to a place (the library, for example) with an arrow and a big text that can be read from far away.
  2. Palette of four. A design with four or more colours where every text passes AA.
  3. Welcome screen. A welcome screen for an event, with big texts and the highest contrast.

Level 5 · No ceiling

  1. Surprise brief. A different brief comes up each time: format, theme, colours and texts. “Another brief” gives you a new one, endlessly.

How contrast is measured

For each text, the studio looks at nine points in its box and works out the colour behind it: the background plus the shapes underneath, with their opacity. It keeps the worst of the nine.

The formula is the one in the WCAG 2.2 guidelines: level AA asks for 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text; level AAA asks for 7:1 and 4.5:1. Large text is size 24 or more, or 18.66 or more in bold.

  • The Atkinson Hyperlegible and Newsreader typefaces load with the page. The others are the system ones.
  • The SVG keeps texts as text: you can open it and keep editing in other drawing programs.
  • Downloaded images carry a strip with the “IRIS GREEN · irisgreen.eu” credit, outside the design.

Your work and your files

What you make starts in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere. If you want to keep it, you can explicitly save it on this device or download your project file.

To carry on another day, press “Save file”: a file with your project downloads to your device. To pick it up again, press “Open file” and choose it. If you close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser warns you.
