School and secondary school: support, adaptations, attendance and exams

Study and workDRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; the final check is still pending.

The LOE/LOMLOE requires specific educational support needs to be identified, resources to be provided and inclusion and non-discrimination to be ensured. Specific supports are also regulated by each autonomous community.

What may be included

Access adaptations, communication supports, changes of location, breaks, anticipation, technology, accessible material, assessment adjustments, guidance-team intervention and individualised measures. The law covers needs related to disability, language and communication disorders, attention or learning, high abilities and other circumstances.

How to ask for it

Request a meeting with the tutor/guidance team and state the barrier: “in written exams they do not finish because of reading speed”, “the dining room triggers overload”, “they cannot answer orally in this situation”. Ask what measure is agreed, who applies it and when it will be reviewed.

School attendance

Persistent attendance difficulties need investigation into what is happening: anxiety, bullying, overload, pain, sleep, learning, transport or conflict. Punishing absence without identifying the barrier can make it worse.

Where it is written

  • Ley Orgánica 2/2006 de Educación, arts. 71–80
  • Ley 15/2022
