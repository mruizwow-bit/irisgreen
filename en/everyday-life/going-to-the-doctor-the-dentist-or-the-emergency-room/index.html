Going to the doctor, the dentist or the emergency room

HealthDRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; the final check is still pending.

A healthcare appointment can be made more accessible when what is going to happen is anticipated and the load of waiting, communication and stimuli is reduced. The person has the right to receive understandable information and, if they have a disability, to the support needed to take part in their healthcare decisions.

What can be requested

The first or last appointment of the day; written instructions; warning before touch; a step-by-step explanation; more time to answer; help from a trusted person if requested; reduced waiting; a less noisy space; and use of the person’s communication method.

Ley 41/2002 establishes that, if the patient is a person with a disability, support measures and information in suitable formats will be offered to help them give consent themselves.

What to bring

A one-page sheet with medication, allergies, communication method, signs of pain, what helps and what worsens the situation. If the person uses AAC, bring it charged and available: it must not be taken away because “today it is time to talk to the doctor”.

In emergency care

Clinical safety comes first. Even so, triage can be told what communication or sensory barriers may make examination difficult and what simple adjustments can make it easier.

Where it is written

  • Ley 41/2002 · patient autonomy
  • Real Decreto 193/2023
