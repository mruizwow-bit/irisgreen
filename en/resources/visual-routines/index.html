Visual routines

A ready-made routine and a builder to make your own. The text always goes with the symbol. There is no correct routine: you can change the order, remove steps or use text only.

The “Morning” routine uses the same eight steps on screen, on A4, on the fridge strip and in “First → Then”. A4 sheets have a single column and a maximum of five steps per sheet.

Prefer a ready-made sheet? Printable routines. Want to practise by playing? Games.

Routine builder

Choose symbols, change the text, reorder with buttons and use the format you need. The library holds 292 pictograms in eleven categories: the same ones used by Games and Printable routines. Nothing is dragged and no account is needed.
