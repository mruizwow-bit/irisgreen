Privacy

What is stored, where it is stored and how to delete it. In short: almost nothing, and the little there is stays in your browser.

What is stored

One thing only: your accessible-reading setting. When you press «more contrast», «more letter spacing» or increase the text size, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage, under the name ig-a11y, so you do not have to press it again on every page.

That setting never leaves your device. It does not travel to any server, it is not linked to your name or your email, and nobody else can read it.

What is not stored

  • No accounts and no passwords: you cannot sign up, because there is nowhere to sign up to.
  • No contact forms on the site.
  • No analytics: nobody measures how many people come in, where from, or what they read.
  • No advertising and no third-party trackers.
  • Nothing is sold, transferred or shared, because there is no data to transfer.

Videos and the playlist

The videos in the library do not load until you press play. Until you do, the page asks YouTube for nothing and YouTube sets no cookies on your behalf. When you press play, the video is embedded from youtube-nocookie.com, the version without advertising cookies. From that moment on, YouTube's own terms apply.

The playlist works the same way: it only loads from Spotify when you open the music panel.

Fonts

The site's typefaces (Newsreader and Atkinson Hyperlegible) are requested from Google Fonts when the page loads. That means Google receives the request for the font file, with the IP address it is requested from, as with any file a browser loads.

How to delete it

  • Press Reset in the accessible-reading panel: that puts the setting back as it was.
  • Or clear the site data in your browser. Clearing «site data» for irisgreen.eu removes it.

Who is responsible

This site is written and maintained by Iris Green. For any privacy question, or to ask for something to be deleted, write to informacion@irisgreen.eu. The person who writes the pages answers.

If this changes

If the site ever adds a form, a search box that records queries, or any measurement, this page will say so beforehand, with the date of the change.
