Your projects

Where your work is kept

While you work, everything stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the workshop does not store it in your browser. To keep it, open File in the studio and press “Save project”: a file downloads to your device. To carry on another day, open that file from File → Open.

No scores or rankings. What you make never leaves your device: you keep it as a file.
