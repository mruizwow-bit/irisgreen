What exists, how to request it and where it is written

Forty-eight practical sheets. Each one addresses a real situation, says what can be requested and links the official source where it is written. At the end of each sheet, the collection concepts that intersect with it are shown.

This information is for guidance only and has no legal validity. For support, deadlines and procedures, always check the linked official source before applying.

Editorial criterion for this collection

When Spanish support, a deadline, a right or a service is mentioned, its source is given. Practical recommendations are presented as supports, not as clinical treatment. A specific adaptation depends on the person and the context; a diagnosis on its own does not automatically grant all the measures described.
