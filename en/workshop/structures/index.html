The workshop · Studio 7

Structures and bridges studio

Build bridges and structures piece by piece and test them: a hanging weight, a car, a lorry or a train. The calculation shows which piece is pulled, which is pushed and which breaks.

  • Table-top model and full size
  • 6 materials
  • Forces and buckling
  • Moving loads
  • Table with every value
  • 11 challenges on 5 levels
  • Mouse, finger or keyboard

How it works

  1. Pick a challenge or set up your own scene under “Scene”.
  2. With “Piece”, join two grid points. Start from the supports at the edges.
  3. Choose the material before placing each piece, or change it later with “Change material” or in the table.
  4. Press “Test the load”. The load crosses the bridge and the drawing shows which piece is pulled, which is pushed and which breaks.
  5. Fix it and test again. Save the project to carry on another day.

The tool needs JavaScript. Here are all the challenges so you can do them on paper or with another tool.

Challenges

The challenges are grouped by level. You can start with any of them.

Level 1 · Triangles (table-top model)

  1. The triangle. Cross a 30 cm gap and hold 1 kg hanging in the middle with five craft sticks at most.
  2. Craft-stick bridge. A 50 cm bridge that holds 2 kg in the middle, with 13 craft sticks at most.

Level 2 · Loads (table-top model)

  1. Five kilos. The same 50 cm gap, now with 5 kg. Craft sticks and string, 21 pieces at most.
  2. Cantilever. There is only a wall on the left. Hold 1 kg 40 cm away from the wall, like a crane or a balcony.

Level 3 · Road (full size)

  1. A car over the river. A 12 m bridge for a car to cross. Seven pieces at most.
  2. A lorry crosses. A 20 m bridge for a 26-tonne lorry. Thirteen pieces at most.

Level 4 · Train (full size)

  1. Let the train through. A 24 m bridge for a locomotive and one wagon to cross: 140 tonnes.
  2. The lightest one that holds a train. The same train and the same gap, with under 2.5 tonnes of structure. Each test shows your best result this session.

Level 5 · No ceiling

  1. Thirty-six metres. A 36 m span with the train. You can use double or triple sections: stronger and heavier.
  2. Arch. A 30 m timber-only bridge for the lorry. You can rest pieces on the canyon walls.
  3. Random commission. A random gap, load and set of materials. Ask for another commission whenever you like: they never run out.

How it calculates

Each piece joins two joints that turn freely, as in a truss. With joints like that, a piece can only pull (tension) or push (compression). The program solves the balance of every joint at once, with the weight of the pieces, the road deck and the load.

A piece breaks if it is pulled harder than it can take, or pushed harder than it can hold. Long pieces that are pushed bend sideways before they are crushed: this is buckling, and it grows with the square of the length. Strings and cables only pull and are fitted taut. If a piece breaks, the calculation removes it and checks whether the rest still holds.

  • It is a model for learning: ideal joints, no wind, no vibration and no safety factors. It is not for designing anything that will be built.
  • Materials (approximate values): 10 × 2 mm craft stick and 5 × 5 mm pine batten (modulus 10 GPa, strength 30 MPa); string that snaps at about 60 N; 20 × 20 cm glued-laminated timber (11 GPa, 24 MPa); IPE 300 steel section (210 GPa, 275 MPa); 50 mm steel cable (design breaking load 1,500 kN).
  • Loads (approximate): 1.5 t car on 2 axles; 26 t lorry on 3 axles; 80 t locomotive and 60 t wagon on 8 axles; road deck of 400 kg per metre at full size.

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the website does not keep it.

To carry on another day, press “Save file”: a file with your project downloads to your device. To pick it up again, press “Open file” and choose it. If you close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser warns you.
