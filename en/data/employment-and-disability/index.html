Employment and disability

61 countriesData from multiple years in 61 countriesDRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; final verification is still pending.

In an ILOSTAT comparison based on 61 countries, around one third of working-age persons with disabilities were employed, approximately half the proportion observed among persons without disabilities. The figure is an unweighted international average, not a single global rate.

The figures

Approximately 1 in 3

working-age persons with disabilities were employed in the ILOSTAT comparative base.

This was approximately half the proportion observed among persons without disabilities.

7 in 10

persons with disabilities were outside the labour force, compared with 4 in 10 persons without disabilities.

What it measures

ILOSTAT compares the labour-market situation of persons with and without disabilities in 61 countries. The average is unweighted: each country contributes to the average without adjustment for population size.

The ILO specifies that these figures are based on the unweighted average of 61 countries. They are not a world rate weighted by population.

How to cite it

The figure comes from the unweighted average of 61 countries. This must be stated explicitly: it is not a population-weighted world rate.

What to avoid

The figure must remain linked to its population, territory, year and method. Separating it from that context can change its meaning.
