The workshop · Studio 13

Robotics studio

A robot with two motors, distance sensors and floor sensors. Program it to leave a room, follow a line, solve mazes it has never seen and work with another robot.

  • Distance and floor sensors
  • New mazes every time
  • Two robots at once
  • Blocks and text
  • Step by step
  • 9 challenges on 5 levels

How it works

  1. Pick a challenge. The scene appears at the top and is described in text below it.
  2. Write the program with blocks or text. It is the same language as in the coding studio, with robot instructions and sensors.
  3. Press “Run” or “Step by step”. The sensors are shown live under the scene.
  4. When it arrives, press “Try it on N variants”: the same program has to work in scenes you have not seen.
  5. In the two-robot challenges, each robot has its own program: switch between “Robot A” and “Robot B”.

The tool needs JavaScript. Here are all the challenges so you can do them on paper or with another tool.

Challenges

The challenges are grouped by level. You can start with any of them.

Level 1 · Moving and measuring

  1. Leave the room. The robot is in a 240 × 110 cm room with a door at the top. Drive it to the goal on the other side of the door.
  2. Park without touching. Drive the robot towards the wall ahead and stop it between 1 and 6 cm away, without bumping. The starting distance changes each time.

Level 2 · Following a line

  1. Follow the line. Follow the black line to the goal using the floor sensors. The curve changes with each variant.
  2. Tight bends. A line with tighter waves. The same program has to arrive on both variants.

Level 3 · Mazes

  1. Known maze. A maze of 6 × 4 cells, each 40 cm. You can see all of it: reach the goal in the top right corner.
  2. Unknown maze. A different maze appears each time. Write a program that reaches the goal in any of them, using the distance sensors.

Level 4 · Several robots

  1. Passing in a corridor. Two robots in a narrow corridor have to swap sides without bumping. There is a bay to pull into. Each robot has its own program.
  2. Meeting point. The two robots start from opposite corners and have to reach the meeting zone in the middle, without bumping into each other or the walls.

Level 5 · No ceiling

  1. Big mazes. Mazes of 10 × 7 cells, each 30 cm, new every time. Try to make your program arrive by the shortest route you can. You only compare yourself with yourself.

Instructions and sensors

Instructions: forward and back in centimetres, right and left in degrees, wait a number of steps. If the robot bumps into something, it stops and bumped() becomes true. Everything else in the language (repeat, while, if, variables, functions) works just as in the coding studio.

  • distance(), distance_left(), distance_right(): free centimetres in that direction.
  • line(), line_left(), line_right(): the three floor sensors, 2.5 cm apart.
  • bumped(), at_goal(), heading().

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the website does not keep it.

To carry on another day, press “Save file”: a file with your project downloads to your device. To pick it up again, press “Open file” and choose it. If you close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser warns you.
