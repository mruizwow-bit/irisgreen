Late diagnosis, self-acceptance, self-advocacy and how to ask for support

RightsDRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; the final check is still pending.

Receiving a diagnostic explanation or identifying with an experience can reorganise years of personal history. The practical part begins by translating that information into concrete needs.

After finding out

It is not necessary to change every routine or tell everyone. Review one situation at a time: work, home, study, health, relationships. Ask: “what is hard for me and what changes if I modify the environment?”

Asking for support

Use a brief formula: barrier + impact + adjustment. Example: “In long meetings I lose information when several people speak; I need an agenda in advance and agreements in writing”.

Self-advocacy

Defending needs does not mean doing everything alone. Someone can be brought to a meeting, a written response can be requested, AAC can be used or accessible information can be requested. Disability legislation recognises reasonable adjustments and accessibility where applicable.
