Labour-force participation and disability

European UnionData from 2024DRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; final verification is still pending.

Participating in the labour market means either working or looking for work. In the European Union, 2024 data show a wide difference between persons with and without disabilities, and especially low participation among people with severe disabilities.

The figures

Using 2024 data:

Just over 56%

of persons aged 20 to 64 with disabilities participated in the labour market.

Compared with:

Approximately 84%

of persons without disabilities.

Among people with severe disabilities, approximately one third participated; among those with a moderate limitation, approximately two thirds did.

What it measures

This indicator measures labour-market participation, that is, being employed or actively looking for employment. A person outside the labour force is not classified in the same way as an unemployed person who is looking for work.

How to cite it

Distinguish labour-force participation from employment: participation means working or looking for work. They are not the same rate.

What to avoid

Labour-market participation and employment are different indicators.

Sources
