The workshop

Projection mapping

Projection mapping projects light onto objects so they seem to change colour, move or tell a story. Here you create surfaces, fill them with content and adjust their corners until they fit the real object.

What you can make here

  • A cardboard box that changes colour
  • A façade or scale model with windows that light up
  • A staircase with animated steps
  • A show with your own images or videos
  • An HTML file to project it from any computer

How it works

  1. Choose an example or press “Add surface”. Each surface is a four-cornered shape.
  2. Drag the numbered circles to place the corners. With the keyboard: Tab to a corner and use the arrow keys (Shift to go faster).
  3. In Properties choose the content: colour, gradient, stripes, waves, text, an image or a video from your device.
  4. Connect the projector as a second screen, move this window there and press “Project”. Turn on the “Alignment pattern” and move the corners until they fit the object.
  5. Save the project and export the show as HTML to play it another time.

What corner pinning is

A projector is almost never straight in front of the object, so the image arrives skewed. Adjusting the four corners warps the image with a perspective transform (homography) so that, seen on the object, it looks straight. It is the same technique that professional mapping programs use.

How to set up a projection

Find a room with little light. Keep the object and the projector fixed: if they move, you have to adjust again. Light, matt surfaces (white card, paper, smooth walls) reflect light best. Start with the alignment pattern: each surface shows a grid and the numbers 1 to 4 in its corners.

No flashing

The animations in this studio change brightness at most once every two seconds, far below the limit of three flashes per second set by the accessibility guidelines (WCAG 2.3.1). If you prefer less motion, animations start paused. If you use your own videos, check that they have no rapid flashes.

Your images and videos

Images are reduced and stored inside the project file. Videos do not fit in the project: they play from your device and you need to choose them again when you open the project or the exported show. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the workshop does not store it in the browser.

To keep it, open File and press “Save project”: a file downloads to your device. To carry on another day, open File, press “Open…” and choose that file. Exports (images, sound, 3D models, games) are also files that you download yourself.
