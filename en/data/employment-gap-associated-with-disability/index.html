Employment gap associated with disability

European UnionData from última serie disponible en la actualización de 2026DRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; final verification is still pending.

In the European Union, the employment gap between persons with and without disabilities was 24.2 percentage points in the latest update published by Eurostat in May 2026.

The figures

24.2 percentage points

Eurostat reported in May 2026 that persons with disabilities had an employment rate 24.2 percentage points lower than that of persons without disabilities.

The data refer to the population aged 20 to 64.

The 2025 edition, with 2024 data, placed the gap at 24.0 points; the 2026 update places it at 24.2 points.

What it measures

The employment gap subtracts the employment rate of persons with disabilities from the employment rate of those without disabilities. The correct unit is therefore percentage points, not a relative percentage.

How to cite it

Use percentage points, not ‘24.2% less’. A gap of 24.2 points compares two employment rates.

What to avoid

A difference expressed in percentage points should not be turned into a relative percentage without recalculating it.
