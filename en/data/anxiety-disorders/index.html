Anxiety disorders

WorldData from 2021DRAFT

Draft page. The sources are named and linked; final verification is still pending.

Anxiety disorders are the most common mental disorders worldwide. WHO estimates that in 2021 they affected 359 million people, including 72 million minors, and that around 27.6% of those who need treatment receive it.

The figures

359 millionpeople with an anxiety disorder in 2021

WHO estimates that:

4,4 %

of the world population currently lives with an anxiety disorder.

Among the 359 million people in 2021 there were:

72 million children and adolescents
Only around 1 in 4 receive treatment

WHO estimates that 27.6% of people who need care for an anxiety disorder receive some treatment.

What it measures

WHO combines a global estimate of people with anxiety disorders in 2021 with an indicator of the treatment gap. The page distinguishes how many people are estimated to be affected from how many receive care among those who need it.

How to cite it

Cite 359 million and 4.4% as WHO estimates for 2021. If the 27.6% figure is used, clarify that it refers to the proportion of people who need treatment who receive some form of treatment.

What to avoid

Anxiety is not a synonym for neurodivergence. It is included because it often appears as a coexisting condition and because it is part of the Iris Green knowledge base.
