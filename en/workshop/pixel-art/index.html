The workshop

Pixel art

Pixel art is drawn square by square, with few colours, like in classic video games. Here you can make characters, objects, repeating backgrounds and small animations.

What you can make here

  • Icons and characters
  • Objects for a video game
  • Tiles that repeat without a visible seam
  • Short animated GIFs
  • Sprite sheets to use in the video game studio or in other engines

How it works

  1. Choose a colour in the palette and paint with the pencil. The first square in the palette is transparent.
  2. Use the bucket to fill areas, and the line or rectangle for straight shapes. With Mirror, what you paint is repeated on the other side.
  3. To animate, add or duplicate frames in Structure. Onion skin shows the previous frame faintly.
  4. Press Animation to see the movement. Change the speed in Properties.
  5. Export from File: a crisp enlarged PNG, a PNG at actual size, a sprite sheet or an animated GIF.

This studio is an interactive tool and needs JavaScript. Below you will find the full explanation of how it works and what you learn.

Fewer colours, more style

Limited palettes make you choose: with 15 colours you have to think about where the light and the shadow go. You can change any colour in the palette and the whole drawing updates.

Frame-by-frame animation

An animation is a series of drawings shown one after another. With 6 to 8 frames per second you can already see the movement. The preview never starts by itself: you start it with the Animation button.

Repeating tiles

Turn on “Show as tiles” to see your drawing repeated around it. If the edges match, it works as a floor or background in a video game with no visible seam.

Export without losing sharpness

When you enlarge pixel art it must be done without smoothing, so each pixel stays a square. The studio’s exports already do that. The GIF keeps transparency and loops.

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the workshop does not store it in the browser.

To keep it, open File and press “Save project”: a file downloads to your device. To carry on another day, open File, press “Open…” and choose that file. Exports (images, sound, 3D models, games) are also files that you download yourself.

How it is made

Libraries are served from irisgreen.eu, with no outside services, and they only load in the studio that uses them. Licences: PixiJS (MIT) and Iris Green’s own GIF encoder · full list of licences.
