The workshop · Studio 10

Circuits studio

A board for building real circuits. In Electricity, the current is calculated instantly: bulbs that shine, LEDs that burn out and motors that turn. In Logic, gates, memory and binary sums.

  • Electricity and logic
  • Live calculation
  • Automatic truth table
  • Flip-flops and clock
  • 14 challenges on 5 levels
  • Accessible parts table

How it works

  1. Choose Electricity or Logic, or a challenge.
  2. With “Place”, choose a part and put it on the board. Join the points with “Wire”.
  3. With “Use”, change switches, changeover switches and inputs. You see the result straight away.
  4. In Logic, the truth table writes itself. With “Clock pulse” the flip-flops move on.
  5. Challenges check themselves while you build. Save the circuit to a file to carry on another day.

The tool needs JavaScript. Here are all the challenges so you can do them on paper or with another tool.

Challenges

The challenges are grouped by level. You can start with any of them.

Level 1 · Electricity: first lights

  1. Light a bulb. A battery, a bulb and wires: make the bulb shine at least halfway.
  2. Two bright bulbs. Two bulbs on a single battery, both at 80% or more.
  3. One switch for two lights. A single switch turns both bulbs on and off together.

Level 2 · Electricity with care

  1. An LED without burning it. Light a red LED with the 9 V battery. On its own it burns out: add a resistor so that 5 to 25 mA flows.
  2. Staircase light. A bulb that goes on and off from downstairs and upstairs: either of the two changeover switches changes it.
  3. A motor both ways. With two changeover switches, make the motor turn one way or the other.

Level 3 · Logic: gates

  1. A and not B. Light S comes on only when A is 1 and B is 0.
  2. Half adder. Add two bits A and B: S is the sum and C is the carry.
  3. Vote of three. Light S comes on when at least two of the three inputs A, B and C are 1.

Level 4 · Logic: memory and sums

  1. Full adder. Add A + B + E (E is the carry coming in from the previous column). Outputs S and C.
  2. Everything from NAND. Exclusive OR (S = 1 if A and B differ) using only NAND gates.
  3. Traffic light. With the clock and two D flip-flops, build a traffic light: each pulse goes from green (G) to amber (A), to red (R) and back to green. Always a single light.
  4. Two-bit adder. Add two two-bit numbers: A1A0 + B1B0 = S2S1S0.

Level 5 · No ceiling

  1. Mystery table. A different three-input truth table appears each time. Build a circuit that matches it. They never run out.

How it calculates

Electricity uses Kirchhoff’s laws and Ohm’s law: the current flowing into each point is the current flowing out, and in each part V = I × R. LEDs are treated as a gate that opens above a certain voltage.

In Logic, each wire is 0 or 1. Gates are worked out until everything is settled; D flip-flops only change on the clock pulse.

  • Approximate values: 1.5 V AA battery, 4.5 V pack of three and 9 V battery, with their internal resistance; 4.5 V 0.3 A bulb; 2 V red LED that burns out above 30 mA; 8 Ω motor.
  • It is a model for learning. It is not for wiring anything to the mains at home: mains voltages are dangerous.

Your work and your files

What you make stays in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere and the website does not keep it.

To carry on another day, press “Save file”: a file with your project downloads to your device. To pick it up again, press “Open file” and choose it. If you close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser warns you.
