The workshop · Studio 1

Drawing studio

A real canvas: layers, brushes, eraser, symmetry and perspective guides. Draw freely or follow challenges that go up in level with no ceiling.

  • 8 tools
  • Layers
  • Symmetry and kaleidoscope
  • 1- and 2-point perspective
  • Series of sheets
  • 15 challenges on 6 levels
  • Mouse, finger, stylus or keyboard

How it works

  1. Pick a challenge or stay in free mode.
  2. Draw with a mouse, your finger, a stylus or the keyboard.
  3. Use layers to keep parts apart: background, figure, details. You can hide them, move them and change their opacity.
  4. Turn on symmetry or a perspective guide when you need it.
  5. Press “Check” to see how the challenge is going. Save a PNG, print, or save the project to carry on another day.

The tool needs JavaScript. Here are all the challenges so you can do them on paper or with another tool.

Challenges

The challenges are grouped by level. You can start with any of them.

Level 1 · First marks

  1. One single line. Draw a face, an animal or an object in one stroke, without lifting the pencil.
  2. Shapes only. Draw an object from your desk using only straight lines, rectangles and ellipses. At least six shapes.
  3. Three colours. A landscape using only three colours. Choose them carefully before you start.

Level 2 · Light and volume

  1. Light in three values. A sphere or a mug with light on it: one light, one middle and one dark value of the same colour.
  2. Three planes. A scene with background, middle ground and foreground, each on its own layer.
  3. Cast shadow. An object and the shadow it casts on the floor, with the light to one side. Write in the alt text where the light comes from.

Level 3 · Symmetry and pattern

  1. Symmetrical butterfly. A butterfly, a mask or a beetle with mirror symmetry.
  2. Eight-fold mandala. A mandala with 8-part radial symmetry, from the centre outwards.
  3. Kaleidoscope. A 6-mirror kaleidoscope with four colours at most.

Level 4 · Perspective

  1. One vanishing point. A corridor, a road or a railway track heading away towards a single point.
  2. Two vanishing points. A box or the corner of a building seen edge-on, with two vanishing points.
  3. Three buildings. A street with three buildings in two-point perspective and a low horizon, as if seen from the ground.

Level 5 · Series with your own rules

  1. The same thing, four lights. The same object on four sheets: morning, midday, evening and night light.
  2. A series of ten with your own rules. Write three rules of your own in “My rules” and make a series of ten sheets that follows them.

Level 6 · No ceiling

  1. Combined challenge. A random subject, limit and technique. Press “Another challenge” as often as you like: they never run out.

What each thing is for

Layers are transparent sheets stacked on top of each other: what you erase on one does not touch the others. Symmetry repeats every stroke as a mirror image or around the centre. Perspective makes lines that go into the distance meet at a point on the horizon, like in a photo of a street.

Sheets are for series: the same drawing in different light, a story in panels or ten variations on one idea.

Your work and your files

What you make starts in this browser tab. It is not sent anywhere. If you want to keep it, you can explicitly save it on this device or download your project file.

To carry on another day, press “Save file”: a file with your project downloads to your device. To pick it up again, press “Open file” and choose it. If you close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser warns you.
